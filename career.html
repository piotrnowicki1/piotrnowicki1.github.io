01 · THE CAREER

The road so far.

Every step taught me a different altitude of the same job: make the thing people need, then make the org that makes the thing.

  1. MAR 2024 — NOW

    Chief Product Officer — Team Internet Group (Commerce Media Tech)

    Ad-tech SaaS: Voluum & Zeropark. Cut 45 scattered initiatives down to 4 core rebuilds and 6 strategic bets, set the group's market positioning, and led its product and AI transformation — while still shipping experimental products myself, on principle.

  2. JAN 2023 — MAR 2024

    Head of Product Management — same company, the fix-the-foundations chapter

    Turned feature factories into teams that own outcomes: product triads, a real user-research culture, separate strategies for both products. Time-to-value down 60%, team eNPS from 20 to 54 — and a promotion to CPO after 14 months.

  3. 2023 — NOW

    Founder — shoshin.pm

    Strategy consultancy run by practitioners who still practice. Workshops that end in decisions, then execution support — for Allegro, Betfan, Kuźniar Media, Valueships, Rohlig SUUS Logistics.

  4. JAN — AUG 2024

    Interim Head of Product — Olly Olly, Charlotte NC (remote)

    The parachute gig: pivoted a marketing agency into a SaaS for US home-service pros. Shipped v1, got the first 1,000 paying customers, built the activation engine, handed over.

  5. JUL 2022 — JAN 2023

    Senior PM / Product Practice Lead — Ramp Network, London

    Clarified the B2B2C Partners strategy into two product lines and shipped the Partners Portal from zero: retention up 12%, issue-resolution time cut ~5×. Led the product-practice transformation on the side.

  6. JUN 2021 — JUL 2022

    Product Manager — Housecall Pro, San Diego

    Owned the calendar — where users spend 90% of their time. Built the area's 3-year strategy, cut lost jobs 6% for the key segment, and sold everyone on an engine rebuild with zero "immediate value" that unlocked everything after. First PM in Poland; hired 3 PMs and 2 designers.

  7. SEP 2020 — JUN 2021

    Product Manager — Delivery Hero (via Netguru)

    Innovation arm of the world's biggest food delivery (2M daily users). White-label groceries engine built in a month, live in Talabat and Hungerstation in two, 20% of early restaurant customers converted to grocery delivery.

  8. MAR 2018 — SEP 2020

    Project / Product Manager — Netguru

    Co-built Netguru's product management practice. Click & collect for Żabka, a US insurtech, a Berlin catering app — alongside the future founder of Gorillas.

  9. ORIGIN STORY

    Production planner — roller blinds, then tires

    Started in the warehouse, got moved to planning, then built an Excel macro that planned the entire production line for me — components, forecasts, output. That was the day I realised I don't want to plan products. I want to build them.
